### 1. Halibut Point

- **Been there?:** Done that.
- **What:** A coastal park and reservation built around the former Babson Farm
  granite quarry, with rocky ledges, tide pools, winding trails, and ocean
  views stretching toward New Hampshire and Maine.
- **Good to know:** The quarry overlook is ADA-accessible, with picnic tables
  and bathrooms nearby. The shore is rocky and requires some clambering; this
  is not a place to swim. Parking costs $5 for Massachusetts residents and $20
  for nonresidents, and fills quickly when the weather is nice. Check the
  timing of low tide to find kelp and critters in tide pools!
- **Area:** Cape Ann
- **Address:** 4 Gott Avenue, Rockport, Massachusetts 01966
- **Map:** https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Halibut+Point+State+Park+4+Gott+Avenue+Rockport+MA
- **Official links:** https://www.mass.gov/locations/halibut-point-state-park;
  https://thetrustees.org/place/halibut-point-reservation/
- **Why:** Deferred.
- **Why-pass notes:** Divorce Day celebration; magnificent kiddo explorations;
  barefoot reminiscences of childhood explorations at Twin Bridge Park;
  birthday group trips; stopping at Woodman's of Essex. Add internal links to
  the future Twin Bridge Park and Woodman's of Essex entries.
