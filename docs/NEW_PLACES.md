## Held discovery: Providence tunnel and circular garden

- **What:** A beautifully lit tunnel leading to a small circular seating area
  enclosed by trees, with an outdoor food-court dining area behind it.
- **Why:** Found by accident after leaving a World Cup watch party while trying
  to busk with a band. The tunnel and hidden “lollipop” clearing felt
  incredible and completely unexpected.
- **Status:** Been
- **Via:** Accidental discovery while playing with a band
- **Area:** Providence, Rhode Island
- **Identity / pin:** Unknown
- **Route clues:** The walk began at a recent World Cup public viewing area,
  continued toward the river, passed a small bank-sponsored stage, and reached
  the tunnel before the circular clearing and outdoor dining area.
